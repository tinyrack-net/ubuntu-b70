# vLLM XPU tuning benchmark — 2026-08-28

Intel Arc Pro B70, `Qwen3.8-27B-AWQ-INT4`, vLLM XPU v0.28.0, 32768-token context.
Each run used five repetitions per prompt/generation case and 256-token requests for concurrency tests.

| Configuration | pp2048 tok/s | tg128 tok/s | tg256 tok/s | C1 output tok/s | C4 output tok/s | C8 output tok/s | C8 median TTFT ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline: graph off, sequences 4, batch auto | 8846.55 | 26.58 | 26.64 | 25.83 | 94.41 | 94.62 | 11769.38 |
| Graph on, sequences 4, batch auto | 6918.32 | 33.55 | 33.47 | 32.12 | 108.17 | 108.21 | 10518.24 |
| Graph on, sequences 8, batch auto | 7566.10 | 33.56 | 33.46 | 32.11 | 108.11 | 177.95 | 1520.42 |
| Graph on, sequences 8, batch 8192 | 8657.98 | 33.56 | 33.50 | 32.10 | 108.21 | 179.51 | 2130.23 |

## Conclusion

- XPU Graph improved single-request generation by about 26%, from 26.6 to 33.5 tok/s.
- Raising `max-num-seqs` from 4 to 8 increased concurrency-8 aggregate throughput from 108.2 to 178.0 tok/s and sharply reduced queueing TTFT.
- Explicit `max-num-batched-tokens=8192` added less than 1% concurrency-8 throughput over auto while increasing median TTFT from 1520 to 2130 ms.
- The recommended throughput configuration is XPU Graph enabled, `max-num-seqs=8`, and automatic batched-token sizing.
- XPU Graph increased cold startup health wait from the baseline behavior to approximately 166–176 seconds in these runs.
